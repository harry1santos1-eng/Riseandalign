How a placement works

Every placement follows the same five steps, whether you are the company hiring or the person being hired.

  1. Brief

    We learn the role, the team, and what success looks like after 90 days.

  2. Shortlist

    You receive vetted profiles, each with a short note on why the person fits.

  3. Interview

    We schedule every round, prepare the candidate, and collect your feedback.

  4. Offer

    We handle references and negotiation so the offer lands cleanly.

  5. Check-in

    We follow up with both sides at 30, 60, and 90 days.

Our standard

Three rules we hold ourselves to on every search.

We say when it is not a fit

If a role or a candidate is wrong, you hear it from us early, before anyone spends weeks on it.

We keep both sides informed

Candidates and companies get clear updates after every step, including when the answer is no.

We stay after the offer

A placement is finished at 90 days, not at signing. Our check-ins are part of the service.
